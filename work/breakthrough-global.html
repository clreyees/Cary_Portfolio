01

Background

Breakthrough Global works with leadership teams at FTSE 100 and Fortune 500 organisations. Its audience is senior, short on time and quick to ignore anything that reads like marketing.

The brief was to build a B2B LinkedIn and thought-leadership presence that would reach that audience directly and position the business as a leader in consultancy. There was already a podcast full of strong ideas, ready to be put to work.

02

Solution

I started with structure: a 30-day and a 90-day content calendar, so every post had a job and a place in a longer story.

The podcast already held the ideas; the job was to cut them into moments a CEO would stop scrolling for. I turned episodes into short-form clips, each carrying a single idea, built for the LinkedIn feed.

Alongside the clips I wrote a post series that set out Breakthrough Global’s point of view, positioning the team as a leader in consultancy.

  • 30 and 90-day content calendars
  • Short-form clips cut from the existing podcast
  • A LinkedIn thought-leadership post series
  • Long-form articles for the Breakthrough Global blog
03

Results

The content reached the people it was written for. Senior management and CEOs engaged with the posts and clips, organic traffic grew by 30%, and enquiries for Breakthrough Global’s services increased.
